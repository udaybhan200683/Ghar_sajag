# C8-P5 — Hub unavailable while Node sleeps

- **Disposition:** BLOCKED / inconclusive. Recovery traffic and eventual durable retirement are observed, but the outage event's exact EventKey and full first-delivery/ACK sequence were not captured; one additional distinct event appeared during recovery.
- **Source and images:** repository source checkpoint `9674075f1911dbad076fdcdb1c541a283dfbb748`; C3 app SHA-256 `1df36cd1a7c779869ea439f2b1e1e45e599c136fd6957ebb59a129870288b814`; S3 app SHA-256 `84358b7deab37c1c66aab3fd134fb23dffd6f497493b806c27accb6b19f56df7`. Existing commissioned diagnostic firmware was reused without erase or reflash.
- **Fixture:** C3 `14:63:93:c5:d1:58`, 4 MiB; S3 `7c:4f:ad:b7:2a:20`, 16 MiB / 8 MiB PSRAM. USB-only power. AM312 at 3.3 V/GND/C3 GPIO4.
- **Baseline:** S3-observed authenticated NodeHealth at `2026-10-14T01:20:34.685260748Z`: session 1, reset 11, retained 0, durable ACK 12, accepted PIR 17, GPIO wakes 17, sleep entries 1020, timer wakes 1003, last wake TIMER.
- **Hub outage:** operator was asked to remove only Hub USB power at `01:21:53.445665007Z`; operator confirmed at `01:22:39.101816345Z`. The stable S3 by-id interface disappeared while C3 remained connected. Power was restored after the motion episode; operator confirmed at `01:25:24.730546619Z`, and S3 returned. Timestamps are in `P5_hub_off_prompt_utc.txt`, `P5_hub_off_operator_done_utc.txt`, `P5_hub_reconnect_prompt_utc.txt`, and `P5_hub_reconnect_operator_done_utc.txt`.
- **Physical motion:** one real motion episode was requested at `01:22:56.028208476Z`; operator replied DONE at `01:23:25.452291743Z`. No cable other than Hub USB was to be touched; no injected motion was used. The C3 native USB console was silent during its light-sleep interval.
- **Capture gap:** the S3 reader was not attached while Hub power was absent and restarted after the Hub returned. Therefore the first reconnect event's raw event/key/retry lines were missed. The authenticated NodeHealth sample at `01:30:39.202349561Z` showed session 2, unchanged reset 11, retained 0, durable ACK 13 (+1), accepted PIR 18 (+1), GPIO wakes 18 (+1), sleep entries 1044 and timer wakes 1026. This supports eventual retirement of one event after the outage, but cannot bind it to an exact EventKey or establish the full retry sequence.
- **Additional recovery event:** at `01:31:37.584567258Z`, Hub log recorded key `p15:c3-146393c5d158n8:gs114-c8s1q14`, session 1 sequence 14, as an authenticated PIR event. First delivery changed durable state (`state_changed=1 records=14 outbox_found=1 admission=OPEN`); a same-key repeat left state unchanged (`state_changed=0 records=14`); RPF00 stayed open and authenticated retirement ACK send returned ESP_OK at `01:31:38.144427658Z`. This proves Hub durable admission and same-key duplicate idempotence for q14. Its relationship to the requested outage stimulus is not established by the missing reconnect capture. A later authenticated NodeHealth sample at `01:36:37.176452138Z` showed session 2, reset 11, retained 0 and durable ACK 14.
- **Verdict:** Do not claim P5 PASS. No Node reset or retained event remained in the available post-recovery health. Exact-key stability from outage through retry, first durable effect, and one-event correlation to the physical stimulus cannot be verified. The q14 additional effect is not enough evidence to identify a firmware defect; retain it as an unexplained stimulus/capture anomaly for a controlled retest.
- **Retest prerequisites:** arm one reconnect-aware S3 reader before removing Hub power; retain the reader through the full outage/recovery interval; record a pre-outage baseline; perform one isolated real motion episode; capture exact EventKey, retry, durable commit, authenticated application ACK and post-ACK NodeHealth. Do not change firmware unless that retest identifies a reproducible defect.
