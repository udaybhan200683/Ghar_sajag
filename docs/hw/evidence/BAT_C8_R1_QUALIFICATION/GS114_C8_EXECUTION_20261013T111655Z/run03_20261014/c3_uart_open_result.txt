Configuration/root-cause check: the maintained C3 HIL sdkconfig defaults select
CONFIG_ESP_CONSOLE_USB_SERIAL_JTAG=y (code/firmware/node/target/esp32c3/idf/
sdkconfig.hil.defaults). Espressif's ESP-IDF ESP32-C3 USB Serial/JTAG guide documents
that APB and USB PHY clocks are gated during light sleep, making the controller
unresponsive to host CDC transactions, and notes that after light sleep the host may
not re-enumerate the device. This matches the observed sleep-time open failure and
stale host-side endpoint. Do not enable USB-connected sleep inhibition for this
qualification: it would prevent the target sleep behavior under test and change its
power profile. Preserve light sleep and use authenticated NodeHealth as the
post-wake observer. Reference: https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/v5.2.1/esp32c3/api-guides/usb-serial-jtag-console.html
